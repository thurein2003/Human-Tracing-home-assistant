# Eye Trace R15 — final two-plate slicer verification

Date: 2026-11-18. Slicer: Creality Print 7.2. Printer: Creality K2. Both original input 3MFs were opened and sliced. No CAD geometry was changed.

## Release verdict

**Overall: HOLD — not print-ready.** Both exact input projects produced completed slices and slicer estimates. Plate 01 remains on HOLD because the captured bridge-height preview does not conclusively show support-interface paths reaching the full underside of the 23.3 mm span. Plate 02 remains on HOLD because Creality Print reports a real first-layer G-code path conflict between generated “Embossed text” and `Pan_KeyedHorn_Cover`.

## Plate 01 — bridge and first-layer inspection

The completed Plate 01 preview was inspected at **Z=19.20 mm**. It shows the internal rectangular structure and actual sliced paths at that height; the global support gap is configured as 0.24 mm with two interface layers. The captured view does not clearly establish a support interface beneath the entire 23.3 mm underside span, so reach and interface contact are **not verified**. The support setting alone is not proof of contact. The base is alone on the plate; its visible brim/support footprint is contained on the bed, and no neighboring part exists to collide. This does not resolve bridge support or confirm first-layer adhesion quality.

## Plate 02 — supports, first layer and clearances

The exact Plate 02 slice completed. Tree supports are generated beneath the large upright yoke/cradle features and are visible in the preview. The packed first-layer footprint includes the intended components and their brims/supports, but the slicer explicitly reports the generated-text collision with the pan horn cover at Z=0.00 mm. Accordingly, no-overlap clearance **fails** for the sliced project as currently loaded; the warning must be cleared and the revised 12-part plate resliced before approval. Do not print this slice.
