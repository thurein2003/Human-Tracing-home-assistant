# Eye Trace R28 single-layer horn mount

R28 uses the R27 FreeCAD assembly and revises only the tilt cradle. The retained upper mounting web is 2.5 mm thick at eight measured locations. A solid comparison found zero geometry difference from the original at and above the web underside. The rest of the camera frame, the 21 × 14 mm camera-hole pattern, the hardware references, their placements, and Plate 01 are preserved.

The continuous lower block was removed. A CAD section below the retained plate contains four isolated fastener pads and zero material outside their local envelopes. The pads connect through the 2.5 mm plate. Two pads also retain the contact faces for the long arms of the keyed SG90 horn. The two others carry clamp load from the existing captive cover. The pads project 4.0 mm locally to reach the original hardware datum, with tip diameters of 6.2 mm on the clamp seats and 7.1 mm on the keyed seats. The region below the web fell from 2,054.06 mm³ to 361.58 mm³, a reduction of 82.4%.

The shaft center remains at assembly Y=0 and Z=78 mm. The four original Ø3.30 mm screw positions and Ø8 mm driver passage are clear. The screw-head bearing faces and modeled M3 screw-to-nut overlap of 2.4 mm are preserved. The provisional horn has approximately 0.10 mm minimum neutral clearance and engages the integrated keys in both relative rotation directions. The installed cradle has zero positive-volume interference at all 19 tested tilt positions from −45° to +45°. The horn and screw references are provisional and physical fit and torque capacity remain untested.

FreeCAD recomputed the assembly and reopened the cradle as one valid solid. The STEP reimport matched the revised solid, and the STL reopened as one watertight mesh. Its triangles match the one-object K2 project. Creality Print opened and sliced the ABS project with tree supports, estimating 3 h 49 min and 31.88 g. The delivered 3MF is the editable slicer project.

The before-and-after screenshots use identical low-side and underside cameras. The installed-horn screenshot shows the provisional horn seated in the integral keys, with the separate captive cover hidden for visibility.
