## Contact classification

- Intentional support: base mounting ears/nut-boss rims, two exterior pan-yoke patches, tilt camera-frame crossbar underside. All generated by slim-tree support in Creality Print.
- Blocked precision regions: horn interfaces, journals/pivot bores, servo screw holes/nut pockets and camera slots. Sampled support centerlines did not enter these areas; the method does not continuously model bead volume.
- Intentional bridges: base wire exit and small horizontal bores. Bridges remain unverified for ABS sag.
- The v5 coupon project has no proximity or outside-bed warning. Coupons were rearranged, not resized.

Actual CAD support-contact images are in RENDERS/Support_Contact_*.png. Actual sliced layers are RENDERS/layer_full_*.png.

Physical ABS fit, shrink, warpage, support removal and strength remain UNVERIFIED.
