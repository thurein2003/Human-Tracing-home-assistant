# Remaining physical verification - ABS

No ABS coupons or parts have been printed. Physical fit is UNVERIFIED.

## Print first
Print FIT_TESTS/STL/EyeTrace_ABS_FitTests_SevenCoupons_FINAL.stl using the v5 coupon project; supports off. Do not print the full 13-part bed first.

## Record
- ABS spool maker/type/batch and temperature guidance; first-layer adhesion and any warp.
- Cool coupons fully, measure before sanding/reaming; record tool resolution, settings, photos, cracking, layer separation, bridge sag and support contact.
- Coupon 01: check both SG90 cases/ears and actual small screws. Do not force M3 through servo ears.
- Coupons 02-03: check both original detached star horns and covers. Original factory screws attach horns to servo shafts; M3 is for printed cover/nuts only.
- Coupon 04: measure four centers; nominal 21 x 16 mm. Test camera screws/shanks without tightening. This does not establish PCB outline, hole diameter, lens or rear-component clearance.
- Coupons 05-06: check pan rings, sliding and female tab bore without force.
- Coupon 07: check actual sleeve/M3 passage and free pivot; not the complete idler stack.

## Before full-size printing
Measure actual camera PCB outline, mounting-hole diameter, lens envelope, nine rear-component heights, connector and cable. Dry assemble both actual SG90s, original horns, pivots, fasteners and camera; check removal access, coaxiality, stops, intended travel and cable routing with motors unpowered. Assess ABS warping and layer strength on the actual K2. No strength certification or powered motion has been done.

Full-print release status remains unchanged.
