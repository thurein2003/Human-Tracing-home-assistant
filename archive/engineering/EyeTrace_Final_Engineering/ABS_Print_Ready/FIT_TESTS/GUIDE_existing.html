# Physical verification: NOT PERFORMED
Print PRINT_FIRST_FitTests_Optimized.stl FIRST, all seven pieces. Existing assembly, validated files and full-print release status remain unchanged. Limits below are proposed engineering fit-screen criteria, NOT published servo tolerances, strength tests or production approval.

Prepare BOTH SG90s/original horns, original factory screws/drivers if available;4 M3x10 +4 M3 nuts (reuse one pair for pivot);2 M1.6x8 +2 nuts +2 small washers; unpowered camera +4 smooth nylon pins. Actual4 M2x12/4 nuts are conditional on real camera holes; do not force them. Add suitable calipers/gauges, ruler/protractor, clean nonconductive support and plastic feelers. Label hardwareA/B. Physically unplug all power. Missing hardware/tool or unmeasured value gives UNVERIFIED, never PASS.

1. SG90 body and mounting ears

Hardware: ServoA thenB; two M1.6x8 screws, two M1.6 nuts, two small washers. Never M3 in original ears.

Method: Unplug power, detach horn, insert case through window until both original ears seat naturally. Fit both small screws without pulling ears into alignment. Finger seat only; repeat on BOTH servos.

  • Printed window23.30x12.80mm and hole pitch27.20mm, each within +/-0.15mm (proposed print-screen limits, NOT SG90 tolerances).
  • Case enters without spreading/scraping/forcing; both flat ear seating faces rest on ledge with no gap >0.15mm. Do not count molded relief as a seating face.
  • Both M1.6 screws enter simultaneously by hand; washers seat and nuts fully engage, with no ear bending/whitening/cracking.
  • This thin gauge does NOT test whole-case height, boss height, bracket captive M1.6 nut pockets, full installation or wire threading. Those stay NOT TESTED.

2. Original star-horn positive pocket

Hardware: Original hornA thenB, DETACHED from servo; paper pointer/ruler/protractor.

Method: Keep cover OFF initially so friction cannot hide a failed profile. Seat detached horn in open pocket. Gently rock between first arm-wall contacts, without flexing arms. Use a paper pointer at20mm radius to measure total free play.

  • Horn seats flat by finger pressure, with no wedged hub, bent arm or raised-hub rocking. Nominal pocket2.20mm deep and2mm horn web are provisional.
  • Positive arm-wall stops in BOTH directions BEFORE clamping. Total free play <=4deg, equivalent to <=1.40mm total arc at20mm radius. Proposed screen criterion, not strength/backlash certification.
  • Check both real horns separately. A five-arm CAD profile is not evidence your original horn matches it.
  • Never force a gearbox or test with a printed spline; no exact spline teeth have been invented.

3. Captive cover and retained horn

Hardware: Coupon02, hornA/B, four M3x10 screws/four standard M3 nuts. Original factory horn screws and correct driver for access comparison only.

Method: Finger-fit nuts in upward-open hex pockets. Put horn in02 and cover FLAT face against open cup, nut bosses OUTWARD. Fit four M3x10 from the opposite face. Seat rims gently and evenly; repeat for other horn.

  • All four nuts fully seat and resist rotation with finger fitting; screws start by hand for >=3 full turns. Do not heat-press nuts or enlarge pockets to call an initial PASS.
  • Rim gap <=0.10mm around perimeter, horn axial lift <=0.20mm, no bowing/crushing; criterion02 still applies independently.
  • Each screw projects >=1.0mm beyond seated nut; nominal2.1mm in this fixture. No head/nut interference.
  • Factory retaining screw HEAD and intended driver clear central8mm fixture access. NEVER substitute M3. Detached clearance is NOT proof of real factory screw thread/length or fully assembled access; record original screw availability separately.

4. Camera21x16 center gauge

Hardware: UNPOWERED camera; four smooth nylon pins, or actual M2x12 only after real PCB holes freely accept the actual shank. Four M2 nuts are for later mount checks, not pulling PCB to gauge.

Method: Measure printed centers first. Hold gauge parallel to camera with VISIBLE clearance from all components/lens. Align four loose nylon pins without clamping. Its central8mm access hole is NOT lens clearance: never lay it over the lens or onto components.

  • Printed horizontal21.00 +/-0.15mm and vertical16.00 +/-0.15mm in a rectangle. Use equal smooth gauge pins: outside span minus mean pin diameter gives centers. User nominal21x16 is authoritative, NOT PCB outline.
  • All four axes align simultaneously without lateral forcing, bending or optical-module contact. Gauge holes2.4mm do not establish actual PCB hole diameter.
  • Actual camera screw shank must pass original hole freely. If M2 does not, mark M2 compatibility FAIL/UNVERIFIED even when pattern aligns. Never drill camera.
  • PCB outline, lens/IR field, rear electronics, standoffs and connector access are NOT passed by this gauge.

5. Male pan journal ring

Hardware: Female ring06, calipers; no servo.

Method: Measure OD at0/45/90deg above first-layer ridge. Mate with LARGE opening of06; slide and turn two full revolutions in EACH direction using light fingers.

  • Male OD64.00 +/-0.15mm; OD ovality max-minus-min <=0.20mm.
  • Minimum female ID minus maximum male OD >=0.30mm; maximum ID minus minimum OD <=0.90mm. Nominal diametral gap0.60mm, radial0.30mm.
  • No press fit, springing, jamming or sticking in two360deg turns each way. Do not selectively sand before recording initial result.
  • 2mm-thin rings test diameter/ovality/sliding, NOT full-height alignment, load, thrust wear or PETG creep.

6. Female pan ring plus tilt-bore tab

Hardware: Male05, actual sleeve07; M3x10/nut may be reused after horn test.

Method: Measure large ID at0/45/90deg and pair with05. The NEW tab contains5.2mm bore through2.5mm thickness, matching nominal stationary tilt wall. Insert07 from UNDER the tab, flange below; neck projects above. See mating-detail CAD image.

  • Pan ID64.60 +/-0.15mm, ID ovality <=0.20mm; meet paired criteria05. Original entire ring material and ID are preserved.
  • Actual small-bore ID minus sleeve neck OD0.10..0.35mm diametral (target0.20mm). Smooth full-length insertion/turning, no forcing.
  • Neck projects >=0.20mm beyond tab face; nominal0.50mm before layer rounding.
  • Only this new coupon copy has the tab. It completes the missing tilt-bore test WITHOUT an eighth piece or assembly change.

7. Actual reusable tilt sleeve

Hardware: Tab06; one M3x10 and standard M3 nut, optional washer. Production idler STILL needs separate M3x12/washer/locknut.

Method: Check actual M3 shank through sleeve. Insert neck through06 from below, flange against underside. With light finger seating, nut/bolt must clamp sleeve end/flange ONLY, never tab. Turn sleeve gently, release nut.

  • Intended neckOD5.00mm, inner bore3.20mm; actual M3 shank passes completely by hand without splitting/crushing.
  • Meet06 diametral gap0.10..0.35mm and end projection >=0.20mm; two full turns EACH direction without binding.
  • Tab remains free when nut bears on sleeve; stop if tightening removes clearance. No tightening-torque or load test.
  • Reuse sleeve only if undamaged. This does NOT pass final idler nut locking, complete stack or bracket alignment.

#8. Check the nine rear-camera uncertainty overlaps without damaging electronics
The red CAD box is UNMEASURED uncertainty, not nine actual camera components. Nine pairs are: plate once, and spacer + M2 fastener at EACH of four holes. No physical clearance has passed.

Disconnect all camera power/USB and servo power. Release real connectors using their own latch, not a tug. Support only safe PCB edges or intended mounting pads on a clean nonconductive surface. Never rest camera on lens, SMD parts or connector. Do not apply metal feelers/caliper jaws to live or fragile electronics. Hidden or unsafe-to-measure regions are UNVERIFIED; tightening is never a clearance probe.

A. Noncontact evidence first: straight-on rear photo showing all four hole centers with external ruler in SAME plane, plus side photo with external scale referenced to REAR PCB face. Measure component heights using an external bridge/reference or safe optical method, never depth-rod force on small components. Record measurement uncertainty u, actual PCB thickness and mounting-pad extents. CAMERA_CLEARANCE_TEMPLATE.svg is an OFF-BOARD/photo overlay only, never press it onto the camera. Print100% and verify20mm scale bar. This is plate geometry, not PCB outline.

B. Pair1, plate: nominal PCB-back to plate-front gap is actual spacer length3mm. At EVERY projected solid plate region (including around central aperture), require component height h + uncertainty u <= ACTUAL spacer length -0.50mm. This0.50mm margin is proposed verification allowance, not manufacturer tolerance. Do not shrink the uncertainty box or call it actual material.

Require full nut engagement and >=1mm thread projection WITHOUT tip interference. Front head needs a safe pad and >=0.50mm component gap. Never rotate test screws against PCB or tighten to flatten a conflict.

#9. Cable/ribbon sweep WITHOUT energizing or backdriving SG90s
Seven coupons alone cannot establish complete assembly cable routing. Use existing safely supported dry-assembly parts or a same-axis fixture. If absent, mark NOT TESTED; do not print full assembly merely to fill this row.

1. Physically unplug BOTH servos and camera power. Unpowered SG90s remain geared: NEVER rotate output by hand, wrench horns or use cable tension to turn them. Removing a center screw alone does NOT disengage a horn from spline.
2. Follow existing service/disassembly guide to physically REMOVE BOTH original horns from motor splines AND printed pockets. Reassemble printed covers/structural joints with pockets EMPTY. Keep motor CASES/wire exits in normal positions. Bare outputs must not rub printed structure. Support the tilt drive end separately to keep the original pivot axis coaxial; opposite journal alone is not a validated complete support. If a safe supported/disengaged setup cannot be made, STOP and mark BLOCKED; never pry gears.
3. This is a cable-route SCREEN, not shaft/torque verification. With horns absent, keep actual connector/lead OUTSIDE the ENTIRE original horn/adapter/shaft regions with >=3mm gap as if those volumes were present. Use strain relief on appropriate structure, not SMD parts. Support camera without loading lens/connector and keep hands away from pinch gaps.
4. Establish geometric neutral using the moving FRAMES and a protractor, not servo spline. CAD zero is not electrical/PWM neutral. Screen pan-55..+55deg, tilt-25..+25deg only; do not extend to guard poses or180deg. Maintain coaxial fixture alignment and record its uncertainty.
5. Move frames VERY slowly and continuously: pan sweeps at tilt-25/0/+25; tilt sweeps at pan-55/0/+55; then both axes between opposite corners. Pause at5deg steps and record at least the15 combinations pan[-55,-30,0,30,55] x tilt[-25,0,25] in CABLE_POSES.csv. Stop at first snag, pinch, tension or contact; do not pull through it.
6. Proposed limited-screen PASS: fully seated connector throughout, no visible connector displacement/tension/rub/crease/witness marks; >=3mm observed gap from moving structures and excluded horn zones, after allowing for fixture-alignment uncertainty; service loop remains visibly curved/slack at EVERY endpoint; measured minimum bend radius >=actual cable maker's relevant static/dynamic bend limit. Compare a smooth radius template BESIDE cable, never forcing cable onto it. Thickness or an arbitrary multiple of it is NOT a manufacturer bend limit. If specification is unknown, geometric observations may pass separately but bend-radius acceptance stays UNVERIFIED.
7. Check strain relief at neutral after sweep. Do not power servos during this stage. Reinstall original horns/screws only using service procedure; do NOT backdrive the restored geared assembly. Powered working travel, real endpoint calibration, payload and repeat use remain unverified. Cable kept clear of horn envelopes during screen still needs final assembled review.

If the lead is not a ribbon, record actual cable type/connector and apply its OWN bend/strain guidance. CAD20x1x18mm reservation proves no real connector or flexible loop. Existing full-print release status is unchanged.
