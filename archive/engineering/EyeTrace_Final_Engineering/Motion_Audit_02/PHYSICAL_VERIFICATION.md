# Physical motion verification checklist

Print only the fit-test coupons before considering the complete ABS bed. Keep servos unpowered for steps 1–6.

- [ ] Both SG90 cases seat in their brackets; ears are flat and mounting screws do not bind or bottom.
- [ ] Original horn center interfaces seat on both output bosses with the original retaining screws; no force, drilling, or guessed spline changes.
- [ ] Pan horn arm is captured by its keyed yoke pocket and cover; gently hand-rotate pan structure and verify positive drive without slip or binding.
- [ ] Tilt horn arm is captured by its keyed cradle pocket and cover; gently hand-rotate cradle and verify positive drive.
- [ ] Opposite-side sleeve and axle hardware retain the cradle while allowing free tilt rotation; tightening does not pinch the stack.
- [ ] Actual camera fits four holes at 21 × 16 mm center-to-center; lens, rear components, connector and screws clear the plate/cradle.
- [ ] Actual ribbon and servo leads remain slack and clear during pan-only, tilt-only, and combined hand sweeps; record safe endpoints.
- [ ] After unpowered checks, energize near neutral and cautiously map pulse to angle for each servo; verify no stalls, horn slip, or cable tension.
- [ ] Record any rub, minimum gap, angle, screw/nut state, and a ruler/caliper photograph for failed interfaces.

**Physical motion status: UNVERIFIED.** CAD kinematics and collision sweeps do not satisfy these checks.
