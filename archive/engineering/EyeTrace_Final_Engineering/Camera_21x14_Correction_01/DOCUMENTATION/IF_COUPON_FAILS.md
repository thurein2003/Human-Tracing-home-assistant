# If the corrected camera coupon fails

Stop if a gauge pin or screw needs force, the PCB flexes, or a pin/screw approaches the lens or components. Do not drill the PCB or alter the corrected nominal pitch from a forced fit.

Send these minimum observations:

1. Coupon horizontal and vertical hole-center measurements (mm), coupon bore diameter, ABS brand, nozzle/bed temperatures and layer height.
2. One sharp front photo showing all four PCB holes and the lens, with calipers/ruler in the image; one rear photo showing all four holes, components and ribbon connector. Keep the camera unpowered and do not place calipers across the lens.
3. Actual camera hole diameter and hole-center spacings measured from the hole centers (or smooth equal pins), plus PCB outside width/height and thickness.
4. For rear clearance only: the maximum protrusion and X/Z location of components near each of the four screw positions; a side photo with a scale is sufficient to identify the highest rear envelope.
5. If only screw clearance fails: screw shank diameter, head diameter/height, nut dimensions, and which mounting direction is available.

A failure could be print shrink/bores, incorrect actual camera pitch, camera hole diameter, outside PCB envelope, screw size, or rear-component interference. These are separate dimensions and will be diagnosed separately.
