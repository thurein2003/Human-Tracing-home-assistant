# Eye Trace R29 integrated mounting plate

R29 replaces the offset web and four hanging cylinders in the R28 tilt cradle with a single 2.5 mm plate. The underside is 4 mm closer to the servo than the R28 web and sits on the existing horn and cover mating plane. This retains the SG90, captive cover, and fastener placements. The recessed horn pocket is cut into the plate. Tapered reinforcement on top rises by up to 1.5 mm to maintain the original screw-head bearing plane, and the keyed recess roof measures at least 1.4 mm at the tested positions.

The vertical frame and upper mount matched the source BREP in the protected region. Plate 01, other components, and all their placements were checked against R28 and preserved. The four screw-head seats and modeled 2.4 mm screw-to-nut overlap were preserved. CAD checks found clearance to the SG90 reference and the provisional horn. The horn pocket reproduced the original bidirectional keyed engagement at the tested relative angles, and the cradle had no positive-volume collision at neutral or the 19 sampled poses from −45° to +45°. Physical horn fit and torque capacity are unverified.

The revised assembly reopened in FreeCAD as one valid solid. The STEP reimport had zero measured solid difference, and the STL reopened as a single watertight mesh with one broad flat bed-contact region. The Creality K2 project contains that same mesh and completed an ABS slice with tree supports. The estimate was 3 h 33 min and 29.42 g. The included 3MF is the editable project.
