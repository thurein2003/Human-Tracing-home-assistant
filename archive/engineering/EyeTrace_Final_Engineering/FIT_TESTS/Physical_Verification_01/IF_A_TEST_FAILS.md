# Minimum evidence ONLY if a test fails or cannot be observed
Identify servo/hornA orB; record filament, actual profile, compensation and finishing. Photos in focus, ruler in SAME plane, never resting ruler on electronics. Keep original parts unmodified.

|Failure|Minimum evidence|
|---|---|
|01 body/ears|Top photo at obstruction and side seating-gap photo; measured printed window/pitch FIRST, then maximum case L/W, ear-hole pitch/diameter, ear thickness and underside seating height at issue.|
|02 star|Straight-on photos of BOTH SIDES of failing detached horn with in-plane scale; arm count/angles/tip extents, web thickness and hub OD/height. No spline reverse-engineering needed if original horn still fits its own servo.|
|03 cover|Gap side photo and nut-seat top photo; horn web/hub height, printed pocket/web dimensions, actual M3 nut AF/height and screw under-head length. For factory screw issue, original head/driver photo and available original length; never guess thread/substituteM3.|
|04 pattern|Perpendicular photo with four holes and gauge held CLEAR of electronics; actual go/no-go pin/hole diameters and printed21x16 distances. Only if disagreement survives print calibration, record actual hole-center coordinates. Do not replace supplied21x16 nominal with PCB outline.|
|Rear camera|Rear photo showing all holes/obstruction, side photo with rear PCB-face reference, h and uncertainty, footprint relative to nearest hole, pad/edge extent, PCB thickness and actual spacer/screw/nut stack. Lens OD/projection/offset or connector envelope ONLY if involved.|
|05/06 pan|OD/ID at0/45/90deg, ring thickness, bed-face photo and tight-point/seam photo; actual print/finishing data.|
|06/07 tilt|Neck OD/bore, tab ID, flange/neck/wall lengths, projection and actual M3 shank; side photo of clamp contact/binding.|
|Cable|Actual type, width/thickness, connector/exit and bend specification if available; neutral and FIRST failing endpoint/corner photos showing angles, route, loop and scale; min gap/radius and confirmation drives were physically disengaged.|

Missing factory screws, unknown holes or unsafe fixture mean UNVERIFIED/BLOCKED, not permission to force fit. No physical result is assumed and full-print release stays unchanged.
