# Remaining physical-fit requirements ? HOLD
User-supplied 21 mm horizontal x 16 mm vertical center spacing is accepted as the nominal camera pattern. It is not the PCB outside size. Do not re-infer this spacing from photographs or PCB dimensions.

Only hardware-dependent evidence remains. See measurement_checklist.svg for datum diagrams, DIMENSIONAL_AUDIT.csv for assumptions and ../FIT_TESTS/INSTRUCTIONS.md for minimal printed checks.

1. For EACH actual SG90: maximum body length/width/height; ear total span, lower seating height and thickness; both hole centers and actual hole diameters/open slots; output-axis offset and boss/shaft height; seated original horn height; real wire exit and connector. Compare with ear coupon. Nominal reference bodies agree with cited SG90 envelopes, but no source certifies these clones or supplies tolerances.
2. For EACH original star horn: arm count/angles/tip outline/widths, web thickness, raised hub and retaining-screw head/length/access when seated. A flat trace with scale or direct coupon result is sufficient for the outer pocket; exact spline teeth need not be reverse-engineered when the original horn stays paired to its servo. Confirm positive wall drive, acceptable lash and axial capture without damage or friction-only torque transfer. Keep original factory retaining screws.
3. Camera: PCB outer extents and hole-center offsets from edges, thickness, actual four hole diameters, lens center/diameter/projection, IR/window field and component heights on both faces. Check the 5 mm standoff footprints at all four holes and the real screws' intrusion. The retained rear electronics uncertainty envelope intersects plate, four spacers and four screws (nine pairs). Measure actual component footprints/heights to resolve this; do not simply hide/delete the box and call clearance verified. Increase real standoffs/revise plate only if evidence requires it, then rerun tests.
4. Cable: actual type, width/thickness, exit and connector, allowed bend radius and free service loop to the controller. Check the full pan/tilt sweep for tension, pinching, abrasion and connector loading. The flat 20 x 1 x 18 reservation proves only empty nominal space. It does not validate a real flexible cable.
5. Hardware and PETG: confirm all actual heads, nuts, washers and tool envelopes. M3 x 10 alone is insufficient: small ear/camera hardware and M3 x 12 idler hardware are additionally specified. Record coupon fits and journal friction/ovality. Test sleeve clamp separation, thrust-ring wear, backlash and fastener retention.
6. Load test: record actual camera/cable mass and center of mass, bracket stiffness and permissible servo torque/duty. Assess stall avoidance, pan support friction and PETG creep/wear at intended temperature. There is no validated payload rating.

Release to full production printing requires recorded PASS results or evidence-based geometry revisions for all critical interfaces, resolution of rear electronics conflicts, successful real cable sweep, correct hardware/tool access and conservative powered functional testing. Any geometry change requires rerunning solid, alignment, all-pair motion, STEP/STL and layout checks. No test-print results are currently recorded.
