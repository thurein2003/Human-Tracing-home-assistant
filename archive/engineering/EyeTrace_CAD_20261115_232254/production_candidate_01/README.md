## Minimal PETG coupon use

Print coupons/coupon_bed.stl only first, 100% scale, millimeters. It has eight distinct test pieces. STL names identify parts; match them to the coupon-bed CAD screenshot. Use your intended nozzle, PETG, layer height and dimensional compensation; do not sand before recording the as-printed result. Use filament-maker settings. The small gauges lie flat and do not require the mechanism's large support structures. FitCoupons_Layout.FCStd shows the packed bed; FitCoupons.FCStd stores individual shapes at their local origins.

1. ServoBodyGauge_24p2x13p2: 6mm-high pass-through gauge with the SAME rectangular opening as the assumed pan case cavity. Slide gently over each real case; record play or interference and actual caliper opening. It checks only the local cross-section, not full insertion, ears, shoulder, cable or retention height.
2. PanHornClamp_Exact and TiltHornClamp_Exact: exact clamp parts oriented flat. Pair each with its corresponding MatingFace_Diagnostic ring and the real horn, using four M3x10 screws and nuts. Ring thickness2 reproduces nominal effective mating grip. The diagnostic rings use an8mm center opening; actual tilt access is7mm and must be checked separately. They omit actual yoke/cradle slots, counterbores and surrounding access walls. Check raised hubs, uniform web contact, seating, bolt intrusion, original center screw access and light hand-torque slip with servo power off. Do not use a destructive torque test. Record how much screw projection remains; no printed result certifies long-term creep.
3. CameraSlotRail_A and B: separate thin rails with40x3.3 slots spanning X5..45 and Y5.35..8.65. Test two pairs of camera holes by positioning rails at the measured row spacing. This is NOT a fixed-pitch mounting template or a camera adapter. Only slot-compatible pitches and diameters can be assessed. At X53, hole gauges at Y3/7/11 have diameters2.2/2.6/3.3 respectively. Use the actual appropriate small camera screw; do not force M3 into smaller holes or let10mm screws contact rear components. Keep the rails off exposed circuitry. Record hole pitch with calipers independently.
4. CameraWidthGauge_38p4: inside jaw span38.4mm, diagnostic for the assumed38mm envelope plus0.4mm total allowance. Check the intended support face without pressing on LEDs or components. Failure means remeasure and redesign; passing does not validate the four-hole pattern.

All coupon solids and individual meshes, plus both combined beds, were audited. Coupons are diagnostic checks and must not be counted among the seven production mechanism parts. After results, update only a NEW production revision and rerun all tests. Do not overwrite this evidence.

## Required before full production printing

- Supply measured values S1-S8, H1-H7, C1-C7 and F1-F2, including both physical servos/horns and all four camera holes; resolve discrepancies against the model.
- Test the coupons in intended PETG and report fit, measured printed sizes and horn stack behavior. Then implement the measured FOUR-HOLE camera attachment, standoffs, component keep-outs and correct screw lengths; strap retention is not a completed four-hole solution.
- Resolve screw-head running clearance, small 1.3mm retainer relief web, 1.5mm clamps, idler anti-loosening, accessible tool paths, real servo ears/cable routing and reliable original center-screw seating.
- Verify camera mass/center of mass and servo duty-cycle/load capacity; test horn slip and PETG creep. The pan bearing and single M3 tilt journal need load/backlash/wear checks.
- Rerun BRep, STL/STEP correspondence, mesh topology, clearances and motion with all measured hardware envelopes and camera holes; inspect slicer supports, brims and layer strength.
- Perform an assembled test print and slow unloaded/loaded motion check with real cables, power/current/temperature monitoring and suitable servo control limits. No mechanical end stops exist. Only then release a full production bed.
