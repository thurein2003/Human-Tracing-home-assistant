# Live demonstration record

The original 31.533-second MP4 is preserved byte for byte at EyeTrace-Live-Demo.mp4 in the local repository workspace. It uses H.264 at 720 × 1280 with AAC audio and is 7,389,979 bytes. The 1-second cropped thumbnail and the four prototype frames were derived from the original. A one-frame-per-second visual review showed the physical rig and workspace, including changes in pan and tilt, the Raspberry Pi and its wiring. The video does not show a verifiable dashboard or independent ground truth for tracking performance.

The cropped stills were inspected and have no source photo metadata. The complete video remains local pending full-resolution label and audio privacy review. The linked [silent preview](EyeTrace-Preview.mp4) copies the original H.264 stream without recompression and drops the audio and source metadata. No external upload occurred.
